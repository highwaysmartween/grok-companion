Nova 1.2.1 - reinstall kit (personal build, keep private)
==========================================================

After reinstalling Windows:

1. Unzip this folder somewhere (e.g. Downloads).
2. Double-click Nova-Setup-Dependencies.cmd and wait until it says "All done".
   (Installs Python, Nova's voice + hearing helpers, the Grok CLI, and restores
   your settings/memory/alarms from the nova-data folder.)
3. Open a NEW PowerShell window and type:   grok login
   Sign in in the browser that opens.
4. Double-click the Nova setup .exe (Grok Companion_1.2.1_x64-setup.exe).
   Done - she starts and remembers you.

No-install option: "Grok Companion 1.2.1 portable.exe" runs without installing
(still do steps 2-3 for voice and brain).

Note: nova-data\settings.json contains your xAI API key. Don't share this zip.
